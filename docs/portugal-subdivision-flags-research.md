# Portugal subdivision flag review

Reviewed 24 August 2026. This review separates flags of the 18 mainland
districts from flags of the same-named capital municipalities. CPBR Atlas keeps
the Portuguese national flag as the mainland-district fallback until a flag can
be tied to the district itself with an authoritative adoption or usage record.

## Finding

- The Portuguese Directorate-General for Local Authorities lists the 18
  districts and the two autonomous regions, but does not publish a district
  symbol or flag field.
- The Wikimedia Commons gallery named “Flags of districts of Portugal” is not
  reliable as an adoption source. Its Aveiro file describes itself as the flag
  used by the *municipality* of Aveiro; Beja explicitly says Beja Municipality;
  Évora explicitly says Évora municipality; and several others are the
  same-named capital-city/municipality flag.
- Flags of the World reports only three scarcely used historical district flags
  (Aveiro, Santarém and Viseu), says Faro almost certainly had none, and cannot
  establish comprehensive or current official adoption. That is useful research
  context, but not strong enough for production assets where correctness is the
  priority.
- The Azores and Madeira are different: both autonomous regions have their own
  legally recognized regional symbols. Their distinct flags remain in the app.
